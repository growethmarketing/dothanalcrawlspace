What Affects the Price?

Crawl Space Size

Encapsulation is priced per square foot — typically $3–$7/sq ft for a full system. Larger spaces cost more; irregular layouts may also affect pricing.

Existing Damage

Mold, standing water, or rotted wood must be addressed before encapsulation. Remediation adds to the total but is necessary for a lasting result.

Access & Clearance

Tight access doors or low-clearance spaces take longer to work in and may cost more. We confirm access conditions during the free inspection.

Components Needed

A dehumidifier and sump pump add cost but are often necessary in Dothan's climate. A free inspection tells you exactly what your home requires.

Repair vs. Encapsulation — Which Is the Better Investment?

If your crawl space has a single, isolated issue in otherwise good condition, targeted repair is often the right call. If moisture or mold is present throughout the space, or you've repaired the same problem more than once, full encapsulation is almost always the better long-term investment — it addresses the root cause rather than the symptoms and lasts 20+ years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does crawl space encapsulation take?
Most residential projects in Dothan take 1–3 days depending on the size and condition of the crawl space.
Do I need a permit for crawl space encapsulation in Alabama?
Basic vapor barrier installation typically does not require a permit. Work involving structural changes or drainage modifications may require one. We confirm permit requirements during the inspection before any work begins.
Full encapsulation or just a vapor barrier — which do I need?
If your crawl space has standing water, active mold, significant moisture damage, or no existing barrier — full encapsulation is the right solution. If moisture is minimal and the space is in good condition, a vapor barrier alone may be sufficient. A free inspection will tell you exactly what your home needs.
Will encapsulation eliminate musty odors in my home?
Yes, in most cases. Musty odors coming from the crawl space are caused by mold and high humidity. Encapsulation removes both conditions and most homeowners notice a difference within weeks of the completed installation.
Is crawl space encapsulation worth the cost?
Yes. A properly encapsulated crawl space lasts 20+ years, reduces HVAC energy costs by up to 20%, improves indoor air quality, and protects your home's structural integrity. Most Dothan homeowners recoup the investment through energy savings and avoided repair costs within 5–7 years.
