Vapor Barriers

The Foundation of a Protected Crawl Space

Without a vapor barrier, ground moisture evaporates directly into the space beneath your home — and from there, into your floors, walls, and the air your family breathes.

We install heavy-duty polyethylene vapor barriers — 12 to 20 mil thick — across your crawl space floor and up the walls, creating a continuous moisture seal that blocks ground vapor from entering your home.

A professionally installed 20-mil vapor barrier lasts 20 years or more. Thinner barriers and DIY installations often fail within 5–8 years — and deteriorating plastic traps moisture against your subfloor rather than blocking it.

What's Included

  • Full crawl space inspection before installation
  • Debris and old barrier removal if needed
  • 12–20 mil reinforced vapor barrier on floors and walls
  • Sealed foundation vents
  • Taped and secured seams for a continuous barrier
  • Optional dehumidifier installation for complete humidity control

Signs You Need a New Vapor Barrier

  • Musty or earthy odors in your home
  • Condensation on pipes, ductwork, or insulation
  • Soft spots or bouncy areas in your floors
  • Visible mold or deteriorating plastic in the crawl space
  • Higher-than-normal energy bills
  • Allergy symptoms that worsen indoors
