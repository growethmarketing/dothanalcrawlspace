Mold Remediation

Mold in Your Crawl Space Doesn't Stay There

Up to 50% of the air in your home flows upward from the crawl space. Mold growing below means mold spores circulating above — affecting air quality and triggering health issues in every room you live in.

Dothan's humidity and clay soil make crawl space mold one of the most common home problems in the area. We see it in homes of every age — from new builds to older properties with decades of moisture accumulation.

Our Mold Remediation Process

  • Full inspection to identify extent and source of mold growth
  • Safe removal of contaminated insulation and debris
  • Treatment of affected wood with professional antimicrobial solutions
  • Encapsulation to seal the crawl space and eliminate moisture
  • Dehumidifier installation to prevent recurrence permanently

Has mold already softened joists or subfloor? Our crawl space repair service handles wood rot and structural fixes.

Mold Always Comes Back Without Encapsulation

Mold remediation without addressing the underlying moisture problem is temporary. We don't just remove the mold — we seal the crawl space so the conditions that allowed mold to grow no longer exist.

Common Signs of Crawl Space Mold

  • Persistent musty odor in your home
  • Unexplained allergy or respiratory symptoms
  • Visible dark staining on wood in the crawl space
  • High indoor humidity even with HVAC running
  • Worsening symptoms when you're home vs. away

Whole-Home Mold

Mold in Your Home Often Starts Below It

If you're seeing mold on bathroom ceilings, closet walls, or around vents, or smelling mustiness you can't trace, the source is often the crawl space. Humid air rises from below into your living space, and cooler surfaces upstairs are where it condenses and grows.

Cleaning visible mold upstairs helps for a while. Lasting mold mitigation means finding where the moisture comes from. We remove the mold in the crawl space, treat affected wood, and seal the space so the mold upstairs doesn't keep coming back.

For heavy mold growth inside living areas, we'll tell you honestly if you need a dedicated indoor mold specialist as well.

Signs Your Mold Problem Starts Under the House

  • Musty smell strongest near floor vents or in rooms over the crawl space
  • Mold returning on walls or ceilings soon after cleaning
  • Indoor humidity staying above 60% even with the AC running
  • Warped flooring or baseboards on the ground floor
