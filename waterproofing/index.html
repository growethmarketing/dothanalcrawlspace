What Causes Water in a Dothan Crawl Space?

Most crawl space water problems in the Dothan area come from three sources: surface water from heavy rain pooling against the foundation, high water table pressure during wet seasons, or plumbing leaks above the crawl space.

We identify the source before we recommend a solution — because treating the symptom without fixing the cause only moves the problem.

Houston County Water Table Facts

Dothan and Houston County receive 58–65 inches of rainfall annually — well above the national average. The area's clay-dominant soil absorbs and holds water rather than draining it, creating persistent hydrostatic pressure against crawl space foundations throughout the wet season.

Foundations & Basements

Foundation & Basement Waterproofing in Dothan

Most Dothan-area homes sit on crawl spaces or slabs. True basements are rare here because of the high water table and clay soil. But the same water that floods crawl spaces also pushes against foundation walls, and homes that do have a basement or below-grade room take on water fast.

We waterproof from the inside, where we can control the result:

  • Interior perimeter drains that relieve water pressure against foundation walls
  • Sump pumps that move collected water away from the house
  • Sealing cracks and gaps where water seeps through block or poured walls
  • Vapor barriers on below-grade walls to stop moisture wicking through masonry
  • Dehumidifiers that hold humidity at safe levels year-round

Signs Water Is Getting Into Your Foundation

  • Stair-step cracks in block walls
  • White, chalky residue on masonry
  • Water marks or dampness along the base of walls
  • Musty smell in below-grade rooms
  • Doors or windows that start to stick

Foundation & Basement Waterproofing FAQ

Do homes in Dothan have basements?
Very few. The high water table and clay-heavy soil make basements expensive to keep dry, so most homes are built on crawl spaces or slabs. When a home does have a basement or below-grade room, it needs active drainage, meaning an interior drain and sump pump, not just sealant.
Can you waterproof a basement without digging outside?
In most cases, yes. Interior drainage and a sump pump handle water that gets through the wall without excavating around the foundation. If exterior work is needed, we'll tell you during the free inspection.
