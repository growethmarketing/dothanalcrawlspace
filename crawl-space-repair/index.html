Something Is Wrong Under Your Home

Maybe you've noticed soft spots in your floors, a persistent musty smell that won't go away, or doors and windows that suddenly don't close right. These aren't random house quirks — they're symptoms of crawl space damage, and they get worse the longer they go unaddressed.

Dothan AL Crawl Space provides professional crawl space repair throughout Dothan, AL and Houston County. We inspect, diagnose, and fix crawl space problems at the source — whether that's moisture damage, wood rot, mold, sagging floors, or a failing vapor barrier.

Common Crawl Space Repairs We Handle

  • Wood rot and joist repair — moisture-damaged floor joists and support beams repaired or sistered before structural integrity is compromised
  • Vapor barrier repair and replacement — failed, torn, or deteriorated plastic replaced with heavy-duty 12–20 mil barriers
  • Drainage and standing water — sump pump installation, French drains, and interior drainage to redirect water away from the foundation
  • Mold treatment — safe removal of contaminated insulation and antimicrobial treatment of affected wood surfaces
  • Crawl space vent sealing — open foundation vents sealed to block humid outside air
  • Insulation removal and replacement — wet or mold-contaminated insulation removed and replaced

Do I Need Crawl Space Repair or Full Encapsulation?

This is the most common question we hear. Here's the honest answer:

Repair is typically right when:

  • You have a specific, localized problem in an otherwise sound crawl space
  • The damage is limited to one area and caught early
  • There's no widespread moisture or mold throughout the space

Encapsulation makes more sense when:

  • Moisture or mold is present throughout the crawl space
  • There's no existing barrier or the existing one has failed
  • You've repaired the same problem more than once
